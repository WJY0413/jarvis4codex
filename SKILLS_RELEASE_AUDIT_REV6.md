# Rev6 bundled Skill release audit

Historical rev6 audit: the results below retain their original revision and do not prove rev9 execution or deployment. The rev9 version-only stage copied all 17 Skill directories/resources from rev8. The later A/B repair updates only current product version labels in the 17 SKILL.md files to 0.3.0; names, control-plane contracts, directory structure and other resources are preserved. Current Skill Creator validation is separately recorded in the A/B handoff. Current inventory/hash readback and selected contract results are indexed in `REV9_DELIVERY_REPAIR_REPORT.md`. Optional integrations remain conditional/unsupported without their separately supplied dependencies; no Skill or optional dependency was deleted to manufacture completeness.

All 17 proposed Skill folders contain one `SKILL.md`; Skill Creator `quick_validate.py` passed 17/17 on the candidate. Their names/frontmatter match their folders. The archive includes no hidden credentials, business queue, private route registry or optional legacy scripts. The loaded MCP registry has 17 public tools; the directly named tool/field shapes below were compared with that registry. `test_027_registry_versions`, `test_public_exact_turn_read`, `test_loop_schema_registry`, and `test_latest_thread_activity` passed 63/63 on rev5 product source. The new disposable official SDK stdio cold probe passed on Windows and WSL: 17 tools, capabilities, preflight, zero model turns. These checks do **not** mean every Skill was used in a real business turn.
